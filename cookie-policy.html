What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember you, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site. Some cookies are essential for the site to work; others are optional and used for analytics or advertising.

Facebook Conversion API (CAPI)

We use server-side tracking only. We do not use the Facebook browser pixel (no fbq() calls in our code). All Facebook tracking happens server-side via the Conversion API, which is more privacy-friendly and bypasses ad blockers.

When you purchase or sign up, our server sends Facebook the following hashed (unreadable) data:

  • Your email address (SHA-256 hashed — Facebook cannot reverse this)
  • Your IP address
  • Your browser user agent
  • The _fbp and fbclid values if available

This data is used solely for measuring the performance of our Facebook advertising campaigns and helping us show our ads to people most likely to be interested in ForgeAI.

Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Fonts to load typography on our site. When your browser requests fonts from Google's servers, Google may set cookies or log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Our payment processors (Cashfree and Gumroad) may also set cookies on their payment pages. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

How to Control Cookies

Browser settings:

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Here's how for common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

Note: Disabling essential cookies (auth tokens) will prevent you from staying logged in to ForgeAI.

Opt out of Facebook advertising:

You can opt out of Facebook interest-based advertising at facebook.com/ads/preferences.
